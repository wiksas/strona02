Privacy policy

This page explains what personal data we process when you use harris.krakow.pl, in particular the reservation form.

Data controller

The data controller is Harris Piano Jazz Bar, Rynek Główny 28, 31-010 Kraków, Poland. For data protection matters write to manager.harris@gmail.com or call +48 12 421 57 41.

Reservation form

The form does not send any data to our server. When you click “Send reservation”, it opens your e-mail app with a ready-made message to rezerwujestolik@gmail.com. We receive your data only when you send that message yourself.

The message contains what you enter: your name, e-mail address, phone number, number of guests, date and time, and any additional information.

  • Purpose: taking and handling your table reservation and contacting you about it.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: as long as needed to handle the reservation; if you withdraw consent, until the message is deleted.
  • Recipients: e-mail is handled by Google's Gmail service, which processes it on our behalf.

Providing data is voluntary, but we cannot take a reservation without it. You can also book by phone.

Cookies and analytics

The website itself does not set cookies or use visitor analytics. Fonts and photos are served from our own server.

The “Find us” section contains an embedded Google map. It loads from Google's servers when that section comes into view, and from then on Google may set cookies and process data (including your IP address) under the Google Privacy Policy.

The concert programme is loaded from the calendar on our own server, harris.krakow.pl.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to the address above; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa).

Changes

The current version of this policy is always available on this page. Last updated: 7 November 2026.
