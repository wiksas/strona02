A jazz club in the heart of Kraków

The second club of its kind in Europe and the only one in Poland where original jazz is played live every single night.

A place with great music, great service, friendly, remarkable people and an atmospheric interior. Harris brings it all together with natural ease, and Rynek Główny 28, right in the heart of the Old Town, has long been a fixture of Kraków's club calendar.

We play jazz in the broadest sense: from the classics, through every kind of blend, to progressive. We are open to other music too, exploratory but always good. Add a fine drink, a flavourful beer or something stronger.

Guests here are close to the musicians, and during jam sessions they often step on stage themselves. Jazz, fusion, latin, funk and soul: bring your instrument or simply listen.

Questions about concerts and bookings

Before you come.

Do I need to book a table?

It is a good idea, because seating is limited, especially for special concerts. We accept reservations by e-mail and by phone (+48 12 421 57 41) until 8 pm.

What time do the concerts start?

Concerts usually start at 9:30 pm. The exact time for each night is listed in the concert programme on this page.

How much is the entry fee?

It depends on the concert and is listed with each event in the programme. Jam sessions usually cost 10 PLN, band concerts most often 25 to 45 PLN.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday from 1 pm, Saturday and Sunday from 12 pm, always until the last guest.

Can I play at a jam session?

Yes. Guests often step on stage during our jam sessions. We play jazz, fusion, latin, funk and soul, so bring your instrument.

Can I eat at Harris?

Yes. The menu includes starters and salads, panini, pizza, pasta and desserts, and the bar serves classic cocktails, draught beer and a wine list.

Where exactly is the club?

At Rynek Główny 28 (Main Market Square) in Kraków, right in the heart of the Old Town.
